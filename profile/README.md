## 🔬 About AIPOCH

We build an AI research workbench and reusable scientific skills to help researchers turn questions into insight. Our work spans research software, medical research skills, and evaluation frameworks.

## AIPOCH Open-Science

Our flagship, open-source AI research workbench for **macOS, Windows, and Linux**.

- **One workspace.** Bring papers, data, Python/R execution, and generated files together.
- **Model choice.** Connect supported providers or a compatible gateway.
- **Inspectable results.** Review available code, execution records, and artifact provenance.
